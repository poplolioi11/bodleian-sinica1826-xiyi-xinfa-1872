# Provenance / 原件與來源

Work: 西醫新法 / Xi yi xin fa [New methods of Western medicine]. Bodleian Libraries, Sinica 1826. Official fixed catalogue supplies [Canton] and [1872]; the original imprint or publisher has not been independently established. Attribution: 美國嘉約翰口譯；莆田林湘東筆述.

- [Digital Bodleian object](https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/objects/d07550d1-b969-4d5a-a5f3-5bd10634b21c/)
- [IIIF manifest](https://iiif.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/iiif/manifest/d07550d1-b969-4d5a-a5f3-5bd10634b21c.json)
- [Fixed official catalogue](https://github.com/bodleian/east-asian-mss/blob/c177d5a6db8585168b744d38a6bff02186d1968f/collections/chinese/Sinica%201826.xml)

The same 25 official 3000-pixel-wide captures underpin v0.1.0 and v0.2.0; `source-pages.json` lists capture hashes, dimensions and links. The transcription scope is 44 numbered physical faces: six figure faces, 37 main-text faces and one original-frame/blank-body face. Four cover surfaces are excluded. The blank body of p44 does not remove its visible margin text 十九.

`source-regions.json` preserves recorded source locators and 15 unresolved edge observations. A face label is an observation locator, not a resolved ownership assignment. No image or crop is redistributed.
