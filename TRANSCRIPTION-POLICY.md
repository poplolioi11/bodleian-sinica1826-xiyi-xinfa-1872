# Transcription policy / 文字政策

以固定初錄為歷史基線，只套用已核定的原圖支持AI修訂；保留原unit ID與順序，不做Unicode正規化或默加現代句讀。原點號可為原刻或後加，精確點碼及來源年代仍未定；疑讀以Unicode碼點0起半開區間定位，未知邊區不猜字數或頁歸屬。

Original unit IDs and order survive revision. Before/after strings and source evidence are fixed privately. No global character or punctuation replacement is used. Source points are approximate encodings; original-print versus later marks remains uncertain. Spacing is a transcription layout representation, not a newly inferred printed character or exact geometric measure.

Uncertainty spans use zero-based, half-open Unicode code-point ranges, not UTF-16 indices. Where primitive deletion/insertion expresses a reorder, a retained uncertainty may explicitly cover the complete selected local replacement. Unknown marginal extent is not rendered as one guessed square per character.
