# 西醫新法（[1872]；Bodleian Sinica 1826） / Xi yi xin fa [New methods of Western medicine]: Transcription dataset

**v0.1.0 | AI initial transcription with uncertainties retained.**

Xi yi xin fa [New methods of Western medicine] is a late-Qing Chinese medical work concerning surgery and bandaging, credited to John Glasgow Kerr (嘉約翰) for oral translation and Lin Xiangdong (林湘東) for written rendering. The official fixed catalogue for Bodleian Libraries, Sinica 1826, supplies [Canton] and [1872]. Derived from the Bodleian digital images, this dataset offers an initial transcription of original printed text across six figure faces, thirty-seven main-text faces, and one face with an original printed frame and a blank body. TXT, JSON, and TEI XML retain readings, uncertainties, and physical-face-to-image mappings for research on late-Qing surgical and bandaging terminology in translation, image-text relationships, and print history. This is an AI initial transcription with uncertainty records; full human double collation and expert review have not been performed.

Modern dataset curator: Chen-Yu Chu (citation form: Chu, Chen-Yu; GitHub account: poplolioi11; [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-9738-6914)). The historical translation attributions identify different roles from modern curation.

## Scope and files

The scope is 44 numbered physical faces p[1]–p[44] within 25 official captures: six figure faces p[1]–p[6], 37 main-text faces p[7]–p[43], and p[44], which retains an original printed frame with a blank body. Four outside/inside cover surfaces are excluded as separate layers. Figure text, captions and original printed paratext are retained as declared in the frozen initial transcription. Figure geometry is not reconstructed. This scope does not establish completeness of the entire original work.

Measured from the frozen initial transcription: 472 units; 9100 Unicode code points; 69 uncertainty records; 15 viewed source marginal regions with uncertain text or page assignment. Code-point counts are not Chinese-character counts, and uncertainty counts are not an error rate.

Files: [TXT](book.txt), [JSON](book.json), [TEI XML](book.tei.xml), [source index](source-pages.json), [uncertainties](uncertainties.json), [source regions](source-regions.json), [data dictionary](DATA-DICTIONARY.md), [transcription policy](TRANSCRIPTION-POLICY.md), [quality](QUALITY.md), [methods](METHODS.md), [corrections](CORRECTIONS.md), [engineering validation](VALIDATION.json), [rights](RIGHTS.md), and [citation metadata](CITATION.cff).

## Source identity

The official fixed catalogue records 西醫新法 and xi yi xin fa, attributes the work to Kerr, John Glasgow, and records 美國嘉約翰口譯 and 莆田林湘東筆述. It supplies [Canton] and [1872]; brackets retain their catalogued supplied status. A handwritten cover date is a later layer and is not treated as an original publication statement. An original imprint and publisher have not been established. [New methods of Western medicine] is an editorial English translation, not an asserted official English catalogue title.

## Quality and use

Independent image reading has not been applied to this version. Full human double collation, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement have not been performed. Original dots may be printed or later additions; this distinction has not been human-verified. Exact JSON/TXT/TEI equivalence, schema checks and checksums do not establish correct readings. Viewed source marginal regions with uncertain text or page assignment retain unknown extent. Some involve whether a marginal strip belongs to the current or adjacent face; they must not all be described as illegible text. They are not unprocessed areas, and an unknown quantity is not replaced with a fabricated single square placeholder. This dataset does not establish that every marginal mark has been assigned to a page or every original character transcribed.

The dataset supports historical-document research. No clinical validation or biological research was conducted. Check quoted text against the fixed version, unit identifier, physical face and official image.

Source images, crops, private programs, prompts and complete private activity/provenance records are excluded. A full image-to-text reproduction method is not provided. Historical public-domain text and faithful mechanical transcription acquire no new exclusive restrictions. Copyrightable original documentation and arrangement use CC BY 4.0. Excluded Bodleian images separately carry CC BY-NC 4.0; see [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md).
